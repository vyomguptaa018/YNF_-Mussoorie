Good to Know

Quick Tips

Small things that make a big difference on a hill trip.

🚗 Getting There

  • Arrive at Dehradun by 5 PM on April 25
  • Cab from Dehradun takes ~1.5 hrs to Mussoorie
  • Fixed price cabs: ~₹600–800 from Dehradun station
  • Avoid arriving on a weekend — roads can be jammed

💵 Money

  • Carry ₹3000+ cash — many spots don't take UPI
  • ATMs on Mall Road (check before going to viewpoints)
  • Bargain at souvenir shops — opening price is 2x
  • Food is cheap; transport adds up fast

🏔️ Altitude & Health

  • Mussoorie is ~2200m — take it easy on Day 1
  • Drink more water than you think you need
  • Headaches are common on Day 1, usually fine by Day 2
  • Carry Dolo/Paracetamol just in case

📱 Connectivity

  • Jio & Airtel have decent coverage on Mall Road
  • Signal drops near viewpoints — download offline maps
  • Download Google Maps for Mussoorie offline before leaving
  • Share your location with someone back home

🌦️ Weather Tips

  • April mornings can be crisp — always carry a jacket
  • Afternoons are warm; evenings cool down fast
  • Light rain is possible — a windcheater doubles as rain cover
  • Best trekking: morning hours before noon

🎒 Trek Days

  • George Everest: wear proper closed shoes, last 15 min steep
  • Carry 1L water per person minimum
  • Start by 9 AM — afternoon mist can reduce views
  • Stock up on Prakash Store snacks before heading out
